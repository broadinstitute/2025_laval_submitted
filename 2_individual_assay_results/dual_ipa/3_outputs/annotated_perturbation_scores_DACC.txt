symbol: (str) HGMD Gene Symbol
orf_id: (int) Internal ORF id
aa_change: (str) Amino Acid change
mut_id: (int) Internal variant id
avg_gfp: (float) Single cells Mean GFP Fluorescence value per well
avg_mcherry: (float) Single cells Mean mCherry Fluorescence value per well
avg_GFP_mCherry_ratio: (float) Ratio of avg_gfp/avg_mCherry
wt_GFP_mCherry_ratio: (float) Ratio of avg_gfp/avg_mCherry of related wild-type measurement
allele_wt_ratio: (float) Ratio of avg_GFP_mCherry_ratio/wt_GFP_mCherry_ratio
dualipa_abun_score: (float) Log2 Z-score of allele_wt_ratio: (log2(allele_wt_ratio) - log2(wt_mean)) / log2(wt_std)
dualipa_abun_change: (str) (Y/N/Uncertain) Significative change of abundance determined by thresholds derived from the log likelihood ratio method (Y/N/Uncertain)
dualipa_abun_chg_dir: (str) (Decrease/Increase) Direction of the abundance change, derived from dualipa_abun_score
